Privacy Policy

Wagmorrow is built to give you a quiet, private place for small daily care. The core experience works without an account, and your personal Wagmorrow content stays on your device.

Who we are

This policy covers the Wagmorrow iOS app and the Wagmorrow website. You can contact the developer at baicuya@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Wagmorrow stores the information needed to run your companion home locally on your iPhone. This may include:

  • your companion’s name, breed, coat, growth stage, and room choices;
  • daily goals, completion dates, Day Fragments, discoveries, seeds, and optional notes;
  • reminder preferences and a minimal product-testing activity log;
  • if you connect a provider, the provider name, provider user identifier, display name, and email address returned during sign-in.

The product-testing activity log contains broad event names, dates, day keys, and goal categories. It does not contain your goal text or notes.

Optional Google or Apple connection

You do not need to connect Google or Apple to use Wagmorrow. Connecting a provider does not create a Wagmorrow cloud account and does not sync or upload your goals, notes, memories, or companion home.

Google

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google processes information needed to authenticate you. Google’s iOS sign-in software may process account information such as your name, email address, user identifier, and authentication data, as well as device, network, coarse location derived from IP address, and usage information for functionality, security, and diagnostics. Wagmorrow stores only the profile fields described above on your device.

Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates you and may return a provider identifier, name, and email address. Apple may let you use a private relay email address. Wagmorrow stores the returned profile on your device.

Google and Apple process information under their own privacy policies. You can use Wagmorrow without either service.

Notifications, backups, and feedback

If you enable a daily reminder, iOS stores and delivers it locally. Wagmorrow does not use a remote push-notification server.

A backup can contain your companion data, goals, memories, and notes. A testing-activity export can contain the activity log described above. These files leave the app only when you start an export and choose a destination through Apple’s system interface.

Feedback text leaves the app only when you choose Share feedback and select a destination. Do not include information you do not want to share.

Tracking, advertising, and sale of data

Wagmorrow does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, behavioral profiling, or a remote analytics service. We do not sell personal information. The Google Sign-In software declares that its data is not used for tracking.

Retention and deletion

Local Wagmorrow data remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or restore a different backup. In the app, open You and choose Delete all local data to remove your Wagmorrow home and locally stored provider profile. Disconnecting Google revokes Wagmorrow’s Google connection and clears the local profile. Signing out of Apple clears the local profile; you can also manage Sign in with Apple authorization in your Apple Account settings.

Exported files are controlled by the destination you chose. Delete those copies from that destination if you no longer want to keep them.

Security and children

Keeping the core experience local reduces the information sent over the internet. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so protect your iPhone with a passcode and store exported backups carefully.

Wagmorrow is a general-audience wellbeing app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for age or date of birth.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Wagmorrow changes. The effective date at the top will show when the current version began. If a future version adds cloud sync, subscriptions, or other server features, this policy and the app’s privacy disclosures will be updated before those features launch.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email baicuya@gmail.com.
