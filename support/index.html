Wagmorrow for iPhone

How can we help?

Most Wagmorrow questions can be solved right in the app. If something still feels wrong, email us and we’ll help.

Getting started

  1. Choose a Golden Retriever or Dachshund and give your companion a name.
  2. On Today, choose one small goal that fits your energy.
  3. Complete the goal to keep one Day Fragment and add energy for an adventure.
  4. Visit Moments from the You screen to revisit your chapters and shared memories.

There are no streak penalties. A day away does not harm your companion or remove progress.

Do I need an account?

No. Every core feature works without signing in. Google and Apple connection are optional and currently keep only a local provider profile. This release does not offer cloud sync.

Sign-in help

Google

Check that your iPhone has an internet connection, then try again from You → Connection. If a browser sheet was closed before sign-in finished, reopen it. Use Disconnect Google to revoke the connection and start over.

Apple

Sign in with Apple requires an active Apple Account on the device. You may share your email or choose Hide My Email. Signing out in Wagmorrow clears the local profile; your goals and memories stay on the device.

Reminders

Open You → A gentle reminder to enable or change the daily reminder. If it does not appear, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Wagmorrow and allow notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally.

Backup and restore

Open You → Your data → Export backup and save the JSON file somewhere you control. To restore, choose Restore from backup, select a Wagmorrow backup, review the summary, and confirm.

Restoring replaces the current home. Keep exported files private because they may contain your goal text and notes.

Delete local data

Open You, scroll to Delete all local data, and confirm. This removes the companion home, goals, memories, settings, and local provider profile from Wagmorrow. This action cannot be undone unless you exported a backup first.
